Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Balavie users can permanently delete their account and associated cloud data from within the application. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed.

Delete your Balavie account

  1. Open Balavie at balavie.ai.studio .
  2. Sign in to the Balavie account you want to delete.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account & Cloud Data.
  5. Review the deletion information displayed by Balavie.
  6. Select Delete Account Permanently.
  7. Complete reauthentication if Firebase requires you to sign in again before account deletion.

Information deleted with your account

A successful account-deletion request permanently removes the following Balavie information:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account
  • Your synchronized Firestore meal-history records
  • Your meal photographs stored in Firebase Storage
  • Your Balavie profile and synchronized settings
  • Your stored age-eligibility confirmation
  • Your Gemini data-processing consent record
  • Your local Balavie meal history and locally retained photographs
  • Your locally stored Gemini API key
  • Your local application preferences and counters
  • Your local Firebase authentication state

Deleting individual meals or photographs

You do not need to delete your complete Balavie account to remove an individual meal.

Open your meal history and use the delete option on the relevant meal. Deleting a meal removes its associated meal record and stored photograph where applicable.

Where the application provides a separate photograph-deletion option, you may delete the photograph while keeping the remaining meal record.

Meal-photograph retention

Meal photographs saved with Balavie meal history are private by default. They are retained for up to six months from their upload date unless you delete them earlier.

At the end of the retention period, Balavie removes the photograph from cloud storage and removes its storage reference. The associated meal-history record may remain unless you delete the meal or your account.

Balavie does not automatically publish or share meal photographs. Any future sharing action must be initiated explicitly by the user.

If you cannot access your account

If you cannot sign in or cannot access the in-app deletion option, contact Balavie support using the email button below.

Use the subject line: Balavie account deletion request

Send the request from the email address associated with the Balavie account, where possible. Do not include your Gemini API key, password, meal photographs, payment-card information, or other sensitive information in the request.

Identity verification

Balavie may need to verify that the person requesting deletion controls the relevant account. Verification is used only to prevent unauthorized deletion requests.

Balavie will never ask you to provide your password or Gemini API key to verify a deletion request.

Deletion processing

In-app deletion should be processed immediately after successful authentication and confirmation. Requests submitted through support will be reviewed and processed as soon as reasonably possible.

Some limited technical records may remain temporarily in secure backups or infrastructure logs until those records are automatically overwritten or deleted according to the relevant service provider’s retention schedule.

Optional donations

Deleting a Balavie account does not automatically delete transaction records held independently by Stripe for an optional donation. Stripe may retain transaction information where required for payment, fraud-prevention, accounting, security, or legal purposes.

Balavie does not store complete payment-card information.

Important

Account deletion is permanent. After deletion, synchronized meals, stored photographs, settings, and account information cannot be recovered through Balavie.

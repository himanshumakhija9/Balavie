Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Balavie users can permanently delete their account and associated cloud data from within the application. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed.

Delete your Balavie account

  1. Open Balavie.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account & Cloud Data.
  5. Review the deletion information.
  6. Select Delete Account Permanently.
  7. Complete reauthentication if requested.
Open Balavie

Information deleted

A successful account deletion removes:

  • The Firebase Authentication account
  • Synchronized Firestore meal records
  • Balavie profile and synchronized settings
  • Age-confirmation and Gemini-consent records
  • Local meal history and IndexedDB photographs on the current device
  • The locally stored Gemini API key
  • Local application preferences and authentication state

Local meal photographs

Meal photographs are stored only on the user’s device using IndexedDB. They are not synchronized to Firestore or any Balavie cloud database.

Deleting a meal removes the associated local photograph from IndexedDB on that device.

Deleting the Balavie account also clears Balavie’s local IndexedDB photo store on the device used to perform the deletion.

Clearing browser data, clearing installed PWA data, or uninstalling the application may also delete locally stored photographs.

Photographs stored on another device must be removed from that device separately because local photographs are not synchronized between devices.

Delete individual meals

Users do not need to delete their complete account to remove an individual meal.

Open meal history and use the delete option for the relevant meal. Deleting the meal removes its synchronized meal record and its associated local photograph from the current device where applicable.

If you cannot access your account

If you cannot sign in or access the in-app deletion feature, submit an account deletion request by email.

Send the request from the email address associated with the Balavie account where possible.

Never include a password, Gemini API key, authentication code, payment-card information, or private meal photograph in the request.

Identity verification

Balavie may need to verify that the requester controls the relevant account to prevent unauthorized deletion.

Balavie will never request a password or Gemini API key for identity verification.

Processing time

In-app deletion should be processed after successful authentication and confirmation. Requests submitted through support will be reviewed as soon as reasonably possible.

Limited technical records may remain temporarily in secure backups or infrastructure logs until automatically overwritten or deleted.

Optional donations

Deleting a Balavie account does not automatically delete transaction records retained independently by Stripe for an optional donation.

Stripe may retain transaction information for payment processing, security, fraud prevention, accounting, or legal purposes.

Important

Account deletion is permanent. Deleted synchronized meals, local photographs, settings, and account information cannot be recovered through Balavie.

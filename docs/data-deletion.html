Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Balavie users can permanently delete their account and associated cloud data from within the application. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed.

Delete your Balavie account

  1. Open Balavie.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account & Cloud Data.
  5. Review the deletion information.
  6. Select Delete Account Permanently.
  7. Complete reauthentication if requested.

Information deleted

A successful account deletion removes:

  • The Firebase Authentication account
  • Synchronized Firestore meal records
  • Meal photographs stored in Firebase Storage
  • Balavie profile and synchronized settings
  • Age-confirmation and Gemini-consent records
  • Local meal history and retained photographs
  • The locally stored Gemini API key
  • Local preferences and authentication state

Delete individual meals or photographs

Users do not need to delete the complete account to remove an individual meal or photograph.

Open meal history and use the delete option for the relevant meal. Deleting a meal also removes its associated stored photograph where applicable.

Photograph retention

Meal photographs stored with Balavie history are private by default and retained for up to six months unless deleted earlier.

After six months, the photograph and its storage reference are removed. The remaining meal-history record may continue to exist until deleted by the user.

Balavie does not automatically publish or share meal photographs.

If you cannot access your account

If you cannot sign in or access the in-app deletion function, contact Balavie support using the subject:

Balavie account deletion request

Send the request from the email address associated with your Balavie account where possible.

Never include your password, Gemini API key, payment-card information, authentication code or private meal photographs in the request.

Identity verification

Balavie may need to verify that the requester controls the relevant account to prevent unauthorized deletion.

Balavie will never request your password or Gemini API key for identity verification.

Processing time

In-app deletion should be processed after successful authentication and confirmation. Requests submitted through support will be reviewed as soon as reasonably possible.

Limited technical records may remain temporarily in secure backups or infrastructure logs until automatically overwritten or deleted.

Optional donations

Deleting a Balavie account does not automatically delete transaction records retained independently by Stripe for an optional donation.

Stripe may retain transaction information for payment processing, security, fraud prevention, accounting or legal purposes.

Important

Account deletion is permanent. Deleted synchronized meals, photographs, settings and account information cannot be recovered through Balavie.

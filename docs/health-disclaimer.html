Health Disclaimer

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Balavie provides approximate nutrition and lifestyle information for educational purposes only. Balavie does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Nutrition estimates

Calories, nutrients, ingredients, portion sizes, Balance Scores, and other information generated by Balavie are estimates.

Results may be incomplete, inaccurate, or unsuitable for an individual user. Text-based and image-based analysis may incorrectly identify foods, portions, ingredients, preparation methods, or nutritional values.

Artificial intelligence limitations

Balavie uses Google's Gemini API to analyse meal descriptions and selected meal photographs using an API key supplied by the user.

Artificial intelligence may produce incorrect, incomplete, outdated, or misleading results. Gemini output must not be treated as professional healthcare or nutrition advice.

Allergies and dietary restrictions

Balavie must not be relied upon to identify allergens or determine whether a meal is safe for an allergy, intolerance, medical condition, or dietary restriction.

Always check product labels, ingredients, preparation methods, and information provided by manufacturers or food-service providers.

Medical and dietary decisions

Consult a qualified doctor, registered dietitian, or other appropriate healthcare professional before making significant changes to diet, activity, medication, or health management.

Professional advice is especially important for:

  • Food allergies or intolerances
  • Diabetes or metabolic conditions
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Eating disorders or disordered eating
  • Medication interactions
  • Nutrient deficiencies
  • Other medical or dietary conditions

No professional relationship

Using Balavie does not create a doctor-patient, dietitian-client, nutritionist-client, or other healthcare-professional relationship.

Medical emergencies

Balavie is not designed for medical emergencies. Contact the appropriate local emergency service if urgent medical assistance is required.

Age requirement

Balavie and its Gemini-powered features are intended only for users aged 18 and over.

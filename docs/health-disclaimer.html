Health Disclaimer

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Balavie provides approximate nutrition and lifestyle information for educational purposes only. Balavie does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Nutrition estimates

Calories, nutrients, ingredients, portion sizes, Balance Scores, and other information generated by Balavie are estimates. Results may be incomplete, inaccurate, or unsuitable for an individual user.

Text-based and image-based analysis may incorrectly identify foods, portions, ingredients, preparation methods, or nutritional values. Important information should always be independently verified.

Artificial intelligence limitations

Balavie uses Google’s Gemini API to analyse meal descriptions and selected meal photographs using an API key supplied by the user.

Artificial intelligence can generate incorrect, incomplete, outdated, or misleading results. Gemini output should not be treated as professional nutrition or healthcare advice.

Allergies and dietary restrictions

Balavie must not be relied upon to identify allergens or determine whether a meal is safe for a particular allergy, intolerance, medical condition, or dietary restriction.

Always check product labels, ingredients, preparation methods, and information provided by food manufacturers or food-service providers.

Medical and dietary decisions

Users should consult a qualified doctor, registered dietitian, or other appropriate healthcare professional before making significant changes to diet, activity, medication, or health management.

Professional advice is particularly important for users managing:

  • Food allergies or intolerances
  • Diabetes or other metabolic conditions
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Eating disorders or disordered eating
  • Medication interactions
  • Nutrient deficiencies
  • Other medical or dietary conditions

No professional relationship

Using Balavie does not create a doctor–patient, dietitian–client, nutritionist–client, or other healthcare-professional relationship.

Medical emergencies

Balavie is not designed for medical emergencies. If urgent medical assistance is required, contact the appropriate local emergency service immediately.

Age requirement

Balavie and its Gemini-powered features are intended only for users aged 18 and over.

Personal responsibility

Users remain responsible for decisions made using information presented by Balavie. Do not disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking professional assistance because of information generated by the application.

Free and open-source project

Balavie is a free and open-source learning project. The application is provided without a guarantee that its nutritional estimates, Balance Scores, or lifestyle suggestions will be accurate, complete, available, or appropriate for every user.
